Launched the first version of an intelligent document management system to automate internal WIs and Checksheets for the engineering team at PT Mipcon Prima Industri.

This first phase release was successfully used by more than 10 senior engineers and field staff. The feedback received was highly positive regarding the application's response speed and intuitive interface. This project demonstrated how transitioning from paper-based to digital-based can save up to hundreds of working hours in a month.
